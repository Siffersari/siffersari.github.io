The problem

Not a screen problem. Connected workflows had drifted apart — money movement, payables and reporting each made sense alone and stopped making sense together, and every new feature widened the gap.

What it provesI can sit on either side of the design–engineering line: audit and redesign a product, then ship it faithfully in someone else's codebase.

What I contributed

  • Audited the product and identified the real problem: connected workflows that had drifted apart
  • Rebuilt the information architecture and navigation so a growing feature set had somewhere to go
  • Took approved designs into production in an established React monorepo, and resolved production UI issues without letting the system drift apart again
›The full record (5 more)
  • Authored desktop and mobile as separate compositions of the same workflow
  • Defined shared component behaviour that holds when reused, rather than per-screen variants
  • Worked the money paths: ACH and direct bank transfers, vendor payments and payables
  • Worked the reading paths: transactions, filters, tags and categories, and the data-dense views around them
  • Card administration: card states, controls, and the operational workflow around them

Decisions that set the scope

  1. 01

    Audited workflows, not screens

    Individual screens were mostly fine. The joins between them were not — a transfer, the transaction it produced and the vendor it belonged to crossed three different mental models.

  2. 02

    Reorganised around what a business does

    Move money, manage who gets paid, read what happened, control the instruments — rather than around the order the features had been built in.

  3. 03

    Defined shared behaviour once

    How a table filters, how a form errors, how a destructive action confirms. Shared behaviour is what stops a redesign decaying into per-screen variants within two quarters.

Approved designs, desktop and mobile

The desktop dialog can afford a sentence per option — "Fund from your external account by ACH or Wire". The phone cannot, so the options are renamed to the shortest form a business still recognises, and the settlement window carries the detail the label gave up. Same three choices, different vocabulary, because the vocabulary had to fit.
