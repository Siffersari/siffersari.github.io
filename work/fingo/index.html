Fingo · Software Engineer · Jan 2021 – Feb 2023

As one of several engineers at Fingo, I built flows where the balance had to be believed.

Transaction reversals and savings pots, on a neobank’s mobile app.

Representative banking surfaces with sample figures: a phone showing an available balance of 12,000 and one card purchase of 1,000, the same purchase shown as the customer’s activity, and the same purchase in a statement with a running balance. The purchase is reversed and every surface shows the same state; then 2,000 is set aside into each of three savings goals in turn — a trip to Mombasa (6,000 to 8,000 of 20,000), tuition (7,000 to 9,000 of 30,000) and a first car (48,000 to 50,000 of 200,000) — leaving 10,000, then 8,000, then 6,000 available. The word Balance is lit in proportion to what remains available. Then a notification, a support chat and a lock screen on the phone.

One reversal. One story everywhere.

Savings pots — the flows customers use to set money aside.
