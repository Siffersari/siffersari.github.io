The problem

Mobile banking lives or dies on correctness. Money has to move, reverse and notify exactly right — and a regulated product cannot cut that corner while the rest of it is still being decided.

What it provesI was trusted with regulated money flows early, in a funded company, and I already cared about the states that only matter when something goes wrong.

What I contributed

  • Savings pots — the flows customers use to set money aside and take it back out
  • Transaction reversals, from the screen a user sees through to the history it has to agree with
  • In-app notifications, and an embedded support chat reachable from onboarding onward
›The full record (4 more)
  • Profile and security flows rebuilt around biometrics and PIN
  • Most of the flows I had worked on, plus onboarding, rebuilt in Flutter/Dart when the app was migrated before launch
  • Product analytics instrumentation, and a proactive outage notification path
  • Shipped continuously while requirements and priorities changed underneath the work

My contribution

2021 — early contribution

A small team, a product still deciding what it was, and regulated money flows built anyway: savings pots, reversals, notifications, support chat, profile and security. Later, before launch, the team moved the app from React Native to Flutter, and I rebuilt most of the flows I had worked on, plus onboarding, in Flutter/Dart.

This is the part I did — both halves of it, alongside other engineers.
