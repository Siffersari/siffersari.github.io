Public Services Platform

Engineering dependable digital workflows for regulated public services.

Agency, domain and operational details are intentionally generalised. This case focuses on my engineering responsibilities, architecture and delivery decisions.

RECORDAUTHORISEDVALIDATION

What I did

  • Held frontend technical ownership across architecture, API integration, delivery and production support on a sensitive public-sector platform.
  • Studied and mapped how the services actually work before writing anything — the regulations, the paperwork, and the people who have to sign.
  • Represented the states a governed record passes through — intake, validation, review and authorisation — including being returned as a normal outcome.
  • Translated complex validation and business rules into dependable multi-step workflows, with asynchronous side effects isolated in Redux-Saga.
  • Supported distinct user roles and decision responsibilities, so the software matches who is actually allowed to decide what.
  • Established reusable components and shared Redux state patterns, so a new record type reuses the workflow instead of duplicating it.
  • Kept the platform adaptable as regulations changed, and gave support teams the visibility to answer a question about any record.

What this provesI can turn regulation and administrative process into software that stays maintainable while the rules keep moving.
