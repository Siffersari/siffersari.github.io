Public Services · National Government collaboration · 2021

One request. Several people decide it.

Dependable digital workflows for sensitive public services. Everyone who acts on a request decides a different part of it — I owned the frontend that gave each of them their part, and kept one record whole between them.

The service

Received.

One record, and everyone who will act on it. Each has a part of their own.
