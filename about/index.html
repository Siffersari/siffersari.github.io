How I work

I build product experiences and the systems that keep them working.

Seven years of production engineering across fintech, telecom, public-service platforms and real-time communication.

I work across frontend architecture, backend and API integration, technical design, testing, releases and production troubleshooting — taking a change from an unclear request through to what happens after it is deployed.

  1. 01

    I build the product, and the system under it

    A messaging SDK other teams install. A neobank's savings and reversal flows. County services turned into workflows that survive their own regulations.

    The interface is what people see; the architecture decides whether it still works next year.

  2. 02

    I own a change end to end

    From an unclear request, through design and trade-offs, into implementation, SIT, UAT and deployment — then monitoring, and whatever comes back.

    Handing over at merge is not ownership.

  3. 03

    I work on both sides of the design line

    I have audited and redesigned a business banking platform in Figma, then delivered that design into someone else's React monorepo without letting it drift back apart.

    Being fluent in both directions removes a whole category of argument.

  4. 04

    Range is a skill, not a logo list

    Fintech, telecom, public services, real-time communication.

    Walking into a regulated, unfamiliar domain and being useful quickly is the job.

Engineering style

Seven years, one direction of travel

Junior engineer in 2019, then frontend architecture and design leadership on a real-time platform, React Native and then Flutter inside a regulated neobank, public-service workflows for a county, a business banking redesign delivered into a team monorepo, and requirements-to-production ownership across enterprise telecom systems.

I build reusable platforms rather than only features, and I am precise about what I can prove versus what I designed. Testing and release validation are how a change earns its way into production — the method, not the whole job.

Client identities, internal systems and customer data for proprietary engagements are intentionally kept private. The engineering value is public; the specifics are shared in interviews.

What this site measures

Privacy-friendly analytics records aggregate visits, referrers, device type and country. It does not use advertising cookies or create personal visitor profiles.

In practice that means a count of which pages and projects were opened, which site or search a visit arrived from, a rough device class and a country — never a name, an email address, an exact location, or anything typed or hovered. No cookie is set and nothing is written to your browser’s storage, so there is nothing here to consent to and nothing to follow you to another site.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, the analytics script is not loaded at all.
